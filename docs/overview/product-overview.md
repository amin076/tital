# Product Overview

Tital is an evidence-governed scientific film direction system. It is designed to assist in the creation of short scientific films by ensuring that all claims made in the film are backed by verifiable evidence and a clear provenance chain.

Tital is not a generic video generator or a general-purpose chatbot. Its core purpose is to guide the creative process of scientific filmmaking through a structured, auditable, and human-in-the-loop workflow.

## Key Features

- **Evidence-Based:** Every claim in a film's script is traced back to a specific source of evidence.
- **Governed Provenance:** A strict chain of domain models ensures that every step of the creative process is recorded and auditable.
- **Human Review Gates:** Human reviewers are an integral part of the workflow, providing necessary oversight and approval at critical stages.
- **Structured Validation:** The system uses Zod schemas to enforce the structure and integrity of all data models.
- **AI-Assisted, Human-Controlled:** Tital uses the Google Agent Development Kit (ADK) and the Gemini family of models to assist with creative tasks, but humans are always in control of the final decisions.

## Target Audience

- **Scientific communicators:** Scientists, educators, and filmmakers who want to create accurate and engaging scientific content.
- **Educational institutions:** Universities and schools that want to produce high-quality educational videos.
- **Research institutions:** Organizations that want to communicate their research findings to a broader audience.
